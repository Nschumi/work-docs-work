# Whitelabel eCommerce

**Type**: Product Area  
**Status**: 🔵 Planned  
**Business Owner**: Commercial Team  
**Technical Lead**: Niclas Schumacher

## Overview

Whitelabel eCommerce is the complete online shopping experience for telecom products and services. It enables customers to browse, configure, and purchase mobile subscriptions, devices, and accessories through a seamless digital journey.

## Business Value

### Revenue Impact
- New digital sales channel
- 24/7 availability
- Reduced store dependency
- Higher conversion rates
- Upsell opportunities

### Customer Benefits
- Convenient shopping
- Transparent pricing
- Easy comparison
- Instant activation
- Self-service options

## Planned Features

### Core Shopping
- Product browsing & search
- Advanced filtering
- Product comparison
- Recommendations
- Wishlist functionality

### Telecom-Specific
- MSISDN selection
- Number portability
- Coverage checking
- SIM options (physical/eSIM)
- Plan configuration

### Purchase Flow
- Shopping cart
- Guest checkout

### Account Integration

- Subscription management
- Digital delivery

## Customer Journey

```
Browse → Select Plan → Choose Number → Configure → 
Cart → Identity Check → Payment → Confirmation → Activation
```
